## Skeleton

- Terrain — SKELETON
- Ocean — SKELETON

- Vegetation / TreeSystem — ACTIVE

## Deep slice

TreeSystem is taken through:

- trunk/branch silhouette,
- bark and wetness response,
- foliage distribution/transmission,
- wind deformation,
- LOD/impostor strategy,
- collision,
- placement rules,
- GPU/memory budget,
- representative render QA,
- standalone demo.

Only after closure may another world system become ACTIVE.
