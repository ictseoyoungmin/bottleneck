# Example — Research

## Skeleton

Create a hypothesis matrix, data provenance map, confounder list, and analysis contract.

## ACTIVE

One falsifiable hypothesis, for example a single variable relationship or causal prediction.

## CLOSED

- assumptions declared,
- statistical/evaluation method fixed,
- analysis reproducible,
- sensitivity/counter-check performed,
- uncertainty reported,
- evidence locations recorded,
- result compressed into a claim capsule.

The rest of the paper narrative remains Skeleton until the evidence units exist.
