# Example — Business Strategy

## Skeleton

- target user,
- core value,
- operating constraints,
- measurable success metrics.

## ACTIVE

“Acquire the first 1,000 qualified users” rather than “finish the business plan.”

Explore alternatives cheaply if needed, select one commitment, then close it with:

- channel mechanics,
- realistic cost assumptions,
- measurable acquisition test,
- adoption/rejection threshold,
- dependencies,
- evidence and unknowns.
